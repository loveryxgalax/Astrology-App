FAQs

Everything customers ask.

Quick answers about Kade Networks internet, installation, payments and support.

How fast can I get connected?

We offer same-day installation in most areas we cover. Once you choose a package, our technician schedules a visit and gets you online, often within hours.

Can I upgrade my package anytime?

Yes. You can move to a faster package at any time. Contact our support team and the upgrade is applied from your next billing cycle, or immediately on request.

Which areas does Kade Networks cover?

We serve homes, businesses and institutions in Ikutha, Kitui County and surrounding areas, and we are expanding. Use the hotspot search above or call us to confirm coverage at your location.

Do you offer 24/7 customer support?

Yes. Our support team is available 24/7 by phone on 0710 810790 and by email at support@kadenetworks.com.

Is installation free?

Yes. Standard installation is free on our internet packages. Extra cabling or special equipment, if required, is quoted upfront before any work starts.

How do I earn loyalty points and vouchers?

You earn Kade Loyalty points every time you pay for your package. Redeem them for data boosts, discounts, free installation, devices and other rewards.
